# Base44 Notes – Torah Tracker

The full write-up is in **TORAH-TRACKER-SPEC.md** (next to this file). Read that.

Raw material from Base44 is in `base44-export/`:
- `src/` – all 45 code files, exactly as Base44 had them
- `entities.json` – the two database tables
- `data/` – your 5 existing rows as CSV
- `chat-history.md` – every message between you and Base44
- `app-meta.md` – app id, routes, login settings
- `assets/` – logo and home-screen screenshot

Captured 2026-09-05 by reading the Base44 editor through the browser. Nothing was changed in Base44.
